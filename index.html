Year by year: duration and actions

Each competition is the final eight only, not the preliminary round.

Duration is the last skill video_time minus the serve video_time. Rows with no skill (rotations, the point code, and other non-skill codes) are left out. All 8,195 rallies are included.

In the men’s European Championship, the bronze match Slovenia–Finland has weaker timing. The correlation of duration with touch count for that whole competition is 0.78. The other five competitions are above 0.92.
