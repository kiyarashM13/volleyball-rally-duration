VNL final eight · 2025 beside 2026

Duration and actions

Women and men stay separate. Each year is eight matches, from the quarterfinals through the final. Rally duration is recorded video time. Actions are the skill count in the same rally.

All six competitions

An action is one skill row: serve, reception, set, attack, block, dig, or freeball. Duration is the last of those video_time values minus the serve video_time.

For the women, mean duration is 6.76 s in 2025 and 6.12 s in 2026, while mean actions are 6.59 and 6.80. For the men, duration stays at 5.06 s and 5.08 s, and actions at 5.41 and 5.45. The spread from match to match is wider than the gap between the two years.
